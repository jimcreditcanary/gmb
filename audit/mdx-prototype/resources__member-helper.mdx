# Member Helper

Gaining control over your finances can be a challenge, but we’re here to help. Discover valuable resources, like our Money Navigator and Budget Planner, to help you manage your money better.
</PageHeader>

<ContentRow colour="white">
<Col>
## How we <u>support you</u>
</Col>
<Col>
We’ve partnered with Money Helper to offer government-backed tools for budgeting and honest debt advice. Become more informed about your financial situation, gain the necessary knowledge to tackle challenges like the cost of living crisis and feel more positive about your future.
</Col>
</ContentRow>

## Budget Planner

Money Helper’s Budget Planner makes managing household spending easier. Get a clear view of your financial situation, learn how to categorise your expenses and ultimately improve your budget. Hundreds of thousands of people have already used the Budget Planner to take back control of their money and move in the right direction.

[Get started today](/resources/member-helper/budget-planner/)
</ColourPanel>
<ColourPanel colour="lilac" image="/media/gmbcu-member-helper-image02.png">
## Cost of Living

We offer valuable insights and resources to help you navigate the increasing cost of everyday life.<br />Discover useful tips, advice and financial help on everything from paying your energy bills to food budgeting. Make better informed decisions about your money with practical information tailored to your circumstances.<br />We’ll help you confidently face the challenges of the rising cost of living and work towards financial stability.

[Learn more](/cost-of-living/)
</ColourPanel>
<ColourPanel colour="ltblue" image="/media/gmbcu-member-helper-image03.png">
## Debt Advice Locator

Money Helper’s Debt Advice Locator connects you with trustworthy free debt advice, including online, telephone and face-to-face support. Access a list of reputable organisations that offer free, confidential, and impartial guidance to help you manage your debts better. Don’t struggle alone with debt. Get support and guidance to regain financial control.

[Learn more](/resources/member-helper/debt-advice-locator/)
</ColourPanel>

## Benefit Calculator

Not sure if you’re entitled to any benefits? Wondering if you are receiving the right amount for your family? Our benefit calculator will help you get all the answers, based on your personal circumstances.

[Learn more](/resources/member-helper/benefit-calculator/)
