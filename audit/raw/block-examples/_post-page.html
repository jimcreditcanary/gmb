2026 AGM Summary

Date: 30 April 2026

On Saturday 18th April, we had the opportunity via Teams to have a roundup of the previous financial year at GMBCU. We covered topics including the GMBCU Prize Draw, business plans and reports from the Board of Directors, Auditors, the Treasurer and more.

We’d like to give a massive thank you to all of those who joined us and pre-submitted their questions. For those who were unable to join us on the day, we have a handy summary of the key takeaways and some further reading for you below.

Annual Dividend

The biggest question on members’ minds at this time of year is the annual dividend. At our AGM, the following tiered rate was agreed upon:

Deferred Shares – 1.00 %
Corporate Savers – 1.00 %
Member Saver – 3.00 %
Young Saver – 4.00 %
Christmas Saver – 4.00 %
Premium Saver – 4.00 %
365 Saver – 5.00 %

This dividend rate was paid to eligible GMBCU member savings accounts on the 22nd April 2026.

The dividend is calculated on the average daily balance of savings in a members’ saving account from 1st January to 31st December 2025.
