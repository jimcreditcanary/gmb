• Personalised, affordable, ethical loans

    We recognise every member has individual financial needs. That’s why we have loans ranging from £200 to £20,000, with rates ranging from 3%-43% APR. We also provide top-ups if you need to borrow a little more by increasing your current loan’s value.

  • Affordable repayments

    Pay interest on the outstanding loan balance. We only charge you interest on how much you have left to pay, not how much you borrowed in the first place. This way we make sure that repayments are more manageable to you.

  • Money saving and budget-friendly

    We help you save money and manage your budget with loans that have no hidden fees or charges for early repayment. This gives you more control, peace of mind and saves you money on interest charges.

  • Flexibility and fair rates

    Most of our loans over £5,000 include a three-month repayment break for unexpected life’s surprises. We also regularly compare our rates and promise to match market prices, helping our members spend less.

  • Locked savings – save as you borrow

    Members with a loan are asked to save a small amount each month or week on top of their loan repayment. Savings are locked and act as security for the loan. Members can withdraw from their savings when their loan balance falls below their savings balance.
