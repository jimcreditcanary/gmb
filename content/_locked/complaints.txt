Complaints Let us put things right. What if I'm unhappy with GMB Credit Union's services? Your satisfaction is our top priority. Please let us know right away if you're unhappy. We welcome all feedback and will try our best to fix any issues. We will also use your input to improve our services for everyone. Our goal is to give GMB Credit Union members the best experience possible. How will we make things right? We'll listen carefully to your feedback, look at the problem in-depth, and take the necessary steps to set things straight. You will hear from us within two working days after submitting a complaint.
