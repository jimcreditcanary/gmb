This month we hosted our 24th Annual General Meeting.

On Saturday 22 April we had the opportunity via Zoom to have a roundup of the previous financial year at GMBCU. We covered topics including the prize draw, business plans and reports from the Board of Directors, Treasurers, Auditors and more.

We’d like to give a massive thank you to all of those who joined us and pre-submitted their questions. However, for those who were unable to join us on the day we have a handy summary of the key takeaways and some further reading for you below.

### Annual Dividend

The biggest question on members minds at this time of year is always the annual dividend. At our AGM, the following tiered rate was agreed upon:

*Corporate Savers 0.75%​*

*Member Saver 0.75%​*

*Junior Saver 1.50%​*

*Christmas Club 1.50%*

This dividend rate was paid to eligible GMBCU member savings accounts on the 24th April 2023.

The dividend is calculated on the average daily balance of savings in a members’ saving account from 1st January to 31st December 2022.

### Q&A with the Treasurer

Thank you to those who submitted questions for our AGM, below is a video covering the Q&A section of the meeting.

David Castledine accompanied us for this session, with topics being covered such as the annual dividend, a look back at 2022, looking forward to the coming 3 years, marketing and communications and product-specific queries.

Watch the Q&A video [here.](https://vimeo.com/819832400?embedded=true&source=vimeo_logo&owner=138541632)

### Annual Report

The Annual Report for year ending 31st December is [published here](/media/GMBCU-Annual-Report-2023-1.pdf) in the interest of transparency.

Expect to see the Treasurer statement, an overview of the operating environment, the auditor’s report, member benefits, and facts and figures relating to the Credit Union’s performance.

It’s a one-stop shop to review our performance and what’s to come. Simple!
