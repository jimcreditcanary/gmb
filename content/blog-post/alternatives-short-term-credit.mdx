For many members accessing short-term credit is unavoidable so if you need money fast, is there another, better way?

In the third and final part of our blog series on short-term credit, we’re exploring alternatives to credit cards, overdrafts, payday loans and buy now, pay later offerings.

If you missed one and two in this series of blogs why not catch up now?

Otherwise, let’s dive straight in.

## **Short-term credit – a necessary part of life?**

It’s not unusual to need a little extra cash to tide you over until payday from time to time. After all, life has a way of throwing financial challenges our way when we least expect them, whether it’s an unexpected car repair, a broken boiler, or that school trip you forgot to factor into your budget.

We understand that smoothing out your cash flow can be difficult and there are times when households need to spread the cost of larger outgoings to balance the monthly budget.

Credit cards, payday loans, and overdrafts, aren’t your only short-term cash option. Indeed, there is much data to support the conclusion that these services are best avoided altogether.

It is possible to access more affordable, flexible and fairer short-term borrowing from a responsible lender who won’t push you outside of your financial comfort zone.

## **GMB Credit Union alternatives to short-term credit**

We want to protect you from the perils of short-term credit because we care about you, your family, and your financial resilience.

In addition to fair and flexible loans, we offer a Cash Flow account which is a pre-agreed overdraft facility for members with a very competitive interest rate and no eye-watering fees.

With a pre-approved credit limit, you can request funds quickly via the GMBCU app and receive the funds on the same day. Plus, the application process does not harm your credit score as we do a soft search on your credit file on application.

Unlike other overdrafts, our Cash Flow overdraft has a fixed monthly repayment, so you stay in control and avoid the temptation to overspend.
