### Benefit from annual returns

We pay an annual dividend on all easy access saving accounts. This dividend is paid out every April and is agreed at our Annual General Meeting (AGM).
</IconItem>
<IconItem icon="/media/Icon-10-Save-Borrow-Afford-48px.svg">
### Save and borrow affordably

When members save with us, we are able to create funds that we pool for lending. This allows us to provide affordable loans to others. Opening a savings account with GMB Credit Union helps build your finances and it also gives you access to lower-rate loans, benefiting everyone involved. This type of credit cycle is known as the virtuous circle.
</IconItem>
<IconItem icon="/media/Icon-11-Protect-48px.svg">
### Protect your money

Looking after your money is our priority. Credit Union savings are fully protected by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) up to £120,000. Rest easy that your savings are safe and secure.
</IconItem>
<IconItem icon="/media/Icon-12-Life-Insurance-48px.svg">
### Safeguard your family with free life insurance

Life insurance cover is provided on all GMB Credit Union member savings accounts up to a maximum of £3,000. This is a free, unique safeguard that supports member’s families with end-of-life expenses. GMBCU has a group insurance policy that automatically insures all our members so there is no paperwork required.
</IconItem>
