# AGM 2025

Our Annual General Meeting to run a member-led discussion about the past year at GMB Credit Union.
</PageHeader>



<PopoutBlock colour="white" image="/media/GMBCU-AGM-2024.png">
## What, where, when?

The AGM is an opportunity for GMBCU members to review reports on the impact of our work over the past year.

Participants will need to read the reports ahead of the meeting to ensure they are informed when casting their vote. Members are also invited to submit any questions they may have in advance before **Wednesday, 26th of March**.

Our Annual General Meeting will be hosted via Zoom on **Saturday,** **29th of March** at **10.00am – 11.00am.** You will need to register ahead of time to attend and can secure your place below.
</PopoutBlock>

<PopoutBlock>
## **AGM On Demand** 

Can’t make it? Don’t worry! We’ll be releasing a video of our Q&A and a summary of the AGM for you to revisit. Think of it as **AGM On Demand,** where you’ll get all the highlights to watch whenever suits you.
</PopoutBlock>
