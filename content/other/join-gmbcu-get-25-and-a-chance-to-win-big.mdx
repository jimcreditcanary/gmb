For Birmingham Equal Pay Claimants

## What’s the offer?

This exclusive savings package has been put together especially for GMB members involved in the Birmingham Equal Pay campaign.

Open a savings account with GMBCU and get £25 to start. That £25 gives you 25 chances to win cash in monthly prize draws – part of our support through the Birmingham Pay Justice campaign.

**Scroll down to apply.**
</PopoutBlock>

<PopoutBlock colour="white" image="/media/Untitled-design-22.png">
## Who we are

**GMB Credit Union (GMBCU)** is run by GMB members, for GMB members. We’re not-for-profit and fully regulated. We help working people save and borrow fairly.

Over the past 25 years, we’ve shared more than **£2 million** in dividends with members. Savings are protected up to **£85,000** by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS).
</PopoutBlock>

If you’re a GMB member in Birmingham, you can open a savings account built to suit real life – whether you need quick access to your money or want to put it aside and leave it. You’ll also get friendly support to help you save with more confidence.
</PopoutBlock>

## A little extra to help you get started

As part of the Birmingham Pay Justice campaign, GMB members in Birmingham who open a Member Saver or Premium Saver account will also **get £25 on us.** This is paid into a separate Prize Saver account opened in your name.

You don’t need to choose the Prize Saver. GMBCU sets it up for you and funds it with £25, so you’re automatically **entered into two monthly prize draws:**

-   A national draw with **a top prize of £5,000**
-   A GMBCU-only draw with **a monthly prize of £500**

Every **£1 saved = 1 entry.** That £25 gives you **25 chances straight away.**

To stay eligible, your Prize Saver needs a minimum balance of £25 (which GMBCU covers to get you started). Max balance is £200. T&Cs apply.
</PopoutBlock>
