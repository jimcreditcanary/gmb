# Join us at Congress 2024

<u>9th – 12th June 2024, Bournemouth&nbsp;</u>
</PageHeader>

<PopoutBlock colour="white" image="/media/GMBCU-PRODUCT-STATIC-1-1.png">
## We are excited to be back at Congress this year.

We are looking forward to having all of our delegates back together in the same building again – to chat face-to-face, share our plans for the future, answer your most pressing questions and – of course – celebrate our 25th anniversary together with everyone.

You can find us at Congress  **Sunday 9th June – Wednesday 12th June 2024** at the **Bournemouth International Centre, Stand 10!**
</PopoutBlock>

<PopoutBlock colour="white" image="/media/May-24-social.png">
## Let’s celebrate 25 years of GMB Credit Union together!

We won’t be hosting Fringe this year but don’t worry – we’ve got some even more exciting news for you. Instead, we will host a reception to celebrate 25 years with us and to find our more about how you can be involved.

The reception will be held on **Sunday 9th June** **at 5.30pm**. Find us in the **Solent Bar & Lounge** for a chat and some celebrations!
</PopoutBlock>
