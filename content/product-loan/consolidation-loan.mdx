<Panel colour="ltblue" title="Your Consolidation loan">
-   £3,000 – £15,000
-   9.4% – 26.8% APR
-   84 months max repayment term
-   Loan repaid by direct debit monthly, four-weekly, or weekly
</Panel>
<Panel colour="ltblue" title="You’re eligible to apply if you…">
-   Are 18 years or over
-   Have been a member of GMB Credit Union for a minimum of six months
-   Can provide proof of ID and regular income
-   Have a good credit rating
-   Agree to keep 10% of your outstanding loan balance in your GMB Credit Union savings account. If not met, you can start saving to meet the requirement. Any savings exceeding the 10% secured savings can be withdrawn
-   Have no County Court Judgements (CCJs), Individual Voluntary Arrangements (IVAs), Debt Relief (DRO) or Bankruptcy
</Panel>
<Panel colour="ltblue" title="Terms & Conditions">
-   No fees or penalties for early repayment
-   Savings are secured until the loan is fully repaid
-   Subject to a credit search and Open Banking
-   We require full details of the existing borrowings to be consolidated including:
    -   Lenders’ name
    -   Type of borrowing
    -   Amount of borrowing outstanding with each lender
    -   Monthly repayment currently made to each lender
    -   It’s the members responsibility to repay the borrowings covered when the consolidation loan is granted
</Panel>
