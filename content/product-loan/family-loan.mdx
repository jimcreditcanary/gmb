<PanelSection>
<Panel colour="ltblue" title="Your Family loan">
-   £200 – £1,200
-   34.5% APR
-   18 months max repayment term
-   Loan repayment is by Child Benefit
-   Fixed repayment of £23 per week or £92 every four weeks
</Panel>
<Panel colour="ltblue" title="You’re eligible to apply if you…">
-   Are 18 years or over
-   Are an active member of the GMB Union
-   Can provide proof of ID and receipt of Child Benefit
-   Agree to have a minimum of £5 in your savings account at all times
-   Have no recent County Court Judgements (CCJs), Individual Voluntary Arrangements (IVAs), Debt Relief (DRO) or Bankruptcy

If you are not a member of GMB Credit Union, we will open a savings account for you as part of the loan agreement process.
</Panel>
<Panel colour="ltblue" title="Terms & Conditions">
-   No fees or penalties for early repayment
-   Subject to a credit search we may request additional information to support your application
-   You are required to retain a minimum of £5 in your credit union savings account
-   Any surplus child benefit funds will be automatically returned to you
</Panel>
