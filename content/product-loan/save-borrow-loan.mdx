<PanelSection>
<Panel colour="green" title="Your Save & Borrow loan">
-   £250 – £2,000
-   42.6% APR
-   12-36 months repayment term
-   Loan repaid by direct debit monthly, four-weekly, or weekly
</Panel>
<Panel colour="green" title="You’re eligible to apply if you…">
-   Are 18 years or over
-   A member of GMB Credit Union
-   Can provide proof of ID and regular income
-   Agree to save a minimum of £25 per month for at least six months before borrowing
-   Have no recent County Court Judgements (CCJs), Individual Voluntary Arrangements (IVAs), Debt Relief (DRO) or Bankruptcy

If you are not a member of GMB Credit Union, we will open a savings account for you as part of the loan agreement process.
</Panel>
<Panel colour="green" title="Terms & Conditions">
-   No fees or penalties for early repayment
-   Loans are based on a maximum of three times your savings
-   You should always have savings equivalent to one-third of your loan balance, which we’ll keep hold of
-   Subject to a credit search and we may request additional information to support your application
</Panel>
