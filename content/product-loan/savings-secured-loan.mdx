<PanelSection>
<Panel colour="ltblue" title="Your Savings Secured loan">
-   £5,000 – £20,000
-   3.5% APR
-   84 months max repayment term
-   Loan repaid by direct debit monthly, four-weekly, or weekly
-   Secure your loan 100% with your savings for the duration of the loan
</Panel>
<Panel colour="ltblue" title="You’re eligible to apply if you…">
-   Are 18 years or over
-   Are a member of GMB Credit Union
-   Have savings equal to or above the loan amount
-   Agree to save a minimum of £10 per month or more
-   Have no recent County Court Judgements (CCJs), Individual Voluntary Arrangements (IVAs), Debt Relief (DRO) or Bankruptcy
</Panel>
<Panel colour="ltblue" title="Terms & Conditions">
-   No fees or penalties for early repayment
-   Savings equivalent to the loan balance are secured until the loan is fully repaid
-   Subject to a soft credit search and Open Banking
-   Members with a loan are asked to save a small amount each month or week on top of their loan repayment. Savings are locked and act as security for the loan. Members can withdraw from their savings when their loan balance falls below their savings balance.
</Panel>
