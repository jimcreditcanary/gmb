<PanelSection>
<Panel colour="ltblue" title="Your Top-Up loan">
-   Loan repaid through your usual direct debit, adjusted to cover the new topped-up amount once agreed
</Panel>
<Panel colour="ltblue" title="You’re eligible to apply if you…">
-   Are 18 years or over
-   Are a member of GMB Credit Union
-   Have an existing GMB Credit Union loan
</Panel>
<Panel colour="ltblue" title="Terms & Conditions">
-   Top Ups are assessed on an individual member basis and are available subject to affordability on all loans except Starter Loans
-   Normally, half of your first loan must be repaid before we can consider a Top Up Loan unless otherwise agreed at the time of the original loan
-   Members can apply for up to three Top Up Loans annually (or a maximum of four Top Up Loans for Family Loans)
-   Subject to a credit search and we may request additional information to support your application
</Panel>
