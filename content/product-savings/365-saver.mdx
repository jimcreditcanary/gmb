<Panel colour="lilac" title="Your 365 Saver">
-   Fixed savings term of 12 months
-   Minimum account balance to open and maintain is £1,000
-   Maximum Account Balance £40,000
-   Lump sums by direct transfer from your bank are welcomed
-   Includes a free life savings cover up to £3,000
-   After a year, enjoy a 5% dividend and automatic transition to our Member Saver account for ongoing saving
</Panel>
<Panel colour="lilac" title="You’re eligible to apply if you…">
-   Are 18 years old or above
-   Are a registered member of the GMB Trade Union
-   Are an account holder with GMB Credit Union
</Panel>
<Panel colour="lilac" title="Terms & Conditions">
-   Internal transfers from existing GMBCU savings accounts are not permitted
-   Withdrawals are not permitted during the 12-month period; if you withdraw, it will cancel any dividend payments
-   Dividends are paid gross. It’s up to members to report this income if they are taxpayers 
-   365 Saver Issue Three will operate from the 01 September 2026 and will mature on the 31 August 2027
-   Deposits are fully protected by the Financial Compensation Scheme (FSCS) up to £120,000
</Panel>
