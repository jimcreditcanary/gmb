<Panel colour="lilac" title="Your Christmas saver">
-   Save weekly or monthly via direct debit or standing order
-   Make one-off deposits from your bank account 
-   Online account management via our App or the My Account area of our website  
-   Savings are accessible to withdraw at your convenience from 1 November until the 31 December.
-   Earn an annual dividend on your savings  
-   Deposits are protected by the Financial Compensation Scheme (FSCS)    
-   Free Life Savings protection up to £3,000
-   Our divided return for 2025 was 4.00%
</Panel>
<Panel colour="lilac" title="You’re eligible to apply if you…">
-   Are 18 years or over
-   Are a GMB trade union member or an immediate family member 
-   Are an existing GMB Credit Union member – Please do not submit an application if you already have a Christmas Saver, please message via Nivo or email to amend your direct debit amount.
</Panel>
<Panel colour="lilac" title="Terms & Conditions">
-   Minimum opening deposit is £5
-   Maximum savings per member is £5,000 
-   To support your Christmas objectives your savings are locked between 1 January and 31 October  
-   Eligible members receive an annual dividend in April, agreed at our AGM\*
-   Dividend is calculated on your daily balance for the year to 31 December
-   We will continue to collect your deposits on the agreed basis in preparation for next Christmas unless otherwise instructed
</Panel>
