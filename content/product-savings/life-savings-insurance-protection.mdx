<Panel colour="lilac" title="The level of insurance cover">
is £3,000 on Member Savings. This means cover will match Member Savings balances up to a maximum of £3,000. In the event of a member’s death, saving deposits made before their 65th birthday will be eligible for 100% insurance cover. Any deposit between the ages of 65-79 will receive 25% insurance cover. Cover will cease on the member’s 80th Birthday.
</Panel>

## Examples of how the scheme supports GMBCU members

**A member dies aged 64 with a savings balance of £3,000 in their Member Savers account.**

-   As £3,000 was held in savings before their 65th birthday, they are eligible for a £3,000 insurance settlement (i.e. 100% cover) doubling their savings to £6,000.

**A member dies aged 70 with a savings balance of £3,000 in their Member Savers account.**

-   As £3,000 was held in savings after their 65th birthday, they are eligible for a £750 insurance settlement (i.e. 25% cover) increasing their savings to £3,750.
</PopoutBlock>
